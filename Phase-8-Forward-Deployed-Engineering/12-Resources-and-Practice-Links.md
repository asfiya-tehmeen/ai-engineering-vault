## Practice opportunities (get real reps)
- **Volunteer/pro-bono AI projects:** nonprofits, schools, local businesses, open-source maintainers needing triage/RAG help — run a real discovery → scope → pilot → handoff cycle.
- **Hackathons & challenges** (lablab.ai, Devpost, provider hackathons) — practice timeboxed delivery and demos.
- **Open-source contributions** to RAG/agent/eval/connector projects — practice reading unfamiliar code and writing clear PRs.
- **Freelance/consulting micro-gigs** (small automation projects) — practice scoping, expectations, communication.
- **Internal projects at your job:** be the "FDE" for another team's workflow; log baseline and results.
- **Role-play partners:** peers, mentors, communities; use LLMs as difficult-customer simulators with detailed personas (and then get human feedback).

## Communities & people to follow
FDE/solutions communities on LinkedIn/X/Substack; AI Engineer conference talks (https://www.ai.engineer/); Latent Space podcast; Hamel Husain, Eugene Yan, Jason Liu, Simon Willison (applied AI practice); company engineering blogs (data/AI platforms and labs); local meetups and alumni networks for informational chats.

## If you only have 1 week
Day 1: read 10 postings + synthesize the role. Day 2: discovery interview (real person) + process map. Day 3: scope + ROI model. Day 4: 48-hour prototype + golden set. Day 5: demo script + weekly-update template; record a demo. Day 6: decomposition drills ×4 + role-play ×2. Day 7: story bank + one full mock round.
